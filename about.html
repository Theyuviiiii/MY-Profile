About

A CSE student who splits time between the browser and the breadboard.

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student at KIET Group of Institutions, currently in my 2nd year, based in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. My main focus is web development — building interfaces and applications with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Firebase — backed by programming fundamentals in C, C++, Java, and Python.

What sets my interests apart is that I don't stop at software. I built an Arduino-based radar scanner from scratch, and I model physical objects in Autodesk Fusion 360 for 3D printing. I like understanding a system end to end — from the sensor reading, to the server, to the screen.

I've also sketched two early product concepts, INTEFY and DIGUP, exploring what a hyperlocal marketplace and a creator portfolio platform could look like. They're ideas-in-progress, not shipped products yet — but they're where a lot of my thinking about product design happens.
